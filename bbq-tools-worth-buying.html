BUYING GUIDE

What BBQ Tools Are Actually Worth Buying?

A useful BBQ setup does not need every gadget on the shelf.

Temperature tools

A reliable instant-read thermometer is useful for checking food quickly. Longer cooks can benefit from a thermometer with a probe designed for monitoring food and pit temperature.

Handling tools

Long-handled tongs, a sturdy spatula, and heat-resistant gloves cover many common grilling and smoking tasks.

Prep gear

A stable cutting board and a good knife are more useful than a drawer full of single-purpose gadgets.

Skip the clutter

Before buying a specialty tool, ask whether it saves meaningful time, improves consistency, or makes a task easier.

Upgrade over time

Start with the tools you use every cook, then add specialty equipment only when a real need appears.
