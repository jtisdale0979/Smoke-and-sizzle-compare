Stocking stuffers (under $50)

A good instant-read thermometer. The single biggest upgrade to anyone’s grilling, and the gift every pitmaster wishes they’d gotten years earlier. If the griller in your life is still cutting meat open to check it, this is the gift.

A solid set of grill tools. Long tongs, a sturdy spatula, and a grill brush that won’t shed bristles into the food. Skip the 20-piece sets — three good tools beat a drawer full of gadgets.

Rubs and sauces from a local maker. A Memphis-style rub or a good sauce is personal, cheap, and always gets used. Bonus points if it’s from a small shop rather than a supermarket shelf.
