BUYING GUIDE

Best Smokers Under $500: What to Compare

Instead of choosing by a feature list alone, compare how each smoker fits the way you actually cook.

Cooking style

Start with how involved you want to be. Pellet smokers automate much of temperature management, while offset and drum designs can involve more hands-on fire management.

Capacity

Think about the amount of food you normally cook and the shape of the cooking chamber. A larger chamber is useful only if you actually need the space.

Temperature control

Look at how the smoker manages airflow and heat. Digital controls can simplify temperature management, while manual designs give you more direct control.

Operating cost

Consider fuel, electricity, pellets, charcoal, wood, replacement parts, and the time required to operate the cooker.

Buying rule

Choose the smoker whose operating style matches how you actually enjoy BBQ. A feature is useful only if it solves a real problem for your cooks.
